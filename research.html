Research Projects

Artificial intelligence, functional neuroimaging, physiological signal reconstruction, and biomedical image analysis.

01 / CEREBROVASCULAR REACTIVITY

AI-Enabled Cerebrovascular Reactivity Estimation from Resting-State fMRI

This project investigates artificial intelligence approaches for estimating cerebrovascular reactivity (CVR) directly from resting-state functional MRI data. By reconstructing physiologically relevant information from routinely acquired imaging measurements, the research aims to reduce dependence on specialized respiratory monitoring and conventional vascular challenge protocols. The longer-term goal is to develop accessible and reproducible computational methods for investigating cerebrovascular function.

Funding: AgeCare Innovation Fund, CAD $10,000 Role: Principal Investigator Period: 2026–2028 Institution: Mount Royal University

02 / PHYSIOLOGICAL SIGNAL MODELING

Sensor-Free Machine Learning for Physiological Correction of fMRI Data

This project develops machine learning approaches for characterizing physiological fluctuations directly from resting-state BOLD fMRI data. The research focuses on respiratory and cardiovascular variability, with the goal of supporting physiological noise modeling and correction without requiring external monitoring equipment. The project also contributes to the development of reproducible computational workflows for physiological signal estimation and neuroimaging analysis.

Funding: MRU Internal Research Grant Fund, CAD $7,000 Role: Principal Investigator Period: 2025–2026 Institution: Mount Royal University

03 / AI AND BRAIN AGING

AI-Driven Estimation of Cardiorespiratory Variability from Brain MRI in Aging

This research investigates the use of artificial intelligence to estimate respiratory and cardiovascular variability from brain MRI data, with particular emphasis on aging populations. The project explores how physiological information embedded in functional neuroimaging signals can be recovered using data-driven computational methods, supporting the development and evaluation of sensor-free physiological modeling approaches across the adult lifespan.

Funding: Alberta Innovates Summer Research Studentship, CAD $7,500 Role: Principal Investigator Year: 2026

04 / DEEP LEARNING FOR NEUROIMAGING

Deep Learning for Physiological Signal Reconstruction Across the Lifespan

This research investigates machine learning and deep learning approaches for reconstructing respiratory and cardiovascular physiological waveforms directly from resting-state BOLD fMRI. The work includes studies involving pediatric, healthy adult, and aging populations, with emphasis on model generalizability, physiological interpretation, and the use of functional and structural brain atlases. The broader objective is to recover meaningful physiological information from neuroimaging measurements without requiring separately acquired physiological recordings.

Research Area: Physiological Signal Reconstruction Methods: Machine Learning and Deep Learning Populations: Children, Adults, and Aging Cohorts

05 / COLLABORATIVE BIOMEDICAL AI

AI for Biomedical Image Reconstruction and Analysis

My collaborative research includes the application of machine learning and deep learning to biomedical imaging problems beyond functional MRI. This work includes image translation approaches for estimating amyloid-beta PET images from structural MRI and deep learning-based denoising methods for reducing radiation dose in computed tomography perfusion imaging. These studies explore how artificial intelligence can improve the utility and accessibility of biomedical imaging.

Research Area: Biomedical Image Analysis Methods: Image Translation and Deep Learning Role: Research Collaborator

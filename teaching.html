Teaching

My teaching focuses on developing students' practical and analytical skills in data science, machine learning, and scientific programming. I emphasize hands-on learning, real-world applications, and problem-solving to help students connect theoretical concepts with practical implementation. Through interactive exercises, programming assignments, and project-based learning, I aim to foster critical thinking, computational skills, and the confidence to tackle complex problems independently.

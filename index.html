About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Data Science at Mount Royal University in Calgary, Canada. My research focuses on developing machine learning and deep learning methods for neuroimaging, physiological signal modeling, and brain health.

I am particularly interested in extracting physiologically meaningful information from functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) and developing computational approaches that advance our understanding of brain function.

Research Areas

Machine Learning for fMRI

Developing computational models to analyze neuroimaging data and uncover meaningful patterns in brain activity.

Physiological Signal Modeling

Estimating respiratory and cardiovascular physiological dynamics from fMRI data.

Brain Health and Aging

Investigating applications of machine learning to brain function, aging, and neurological health.

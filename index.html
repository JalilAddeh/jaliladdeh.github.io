About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Data Science at Mount Royal University in Calgary, Canada. My research focuses on developing machine learning and deep learning approaches for neuroimaging and physiological signal modeling.

My work investigates how physiological information can be extracted from functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI), with particular interest in respiratory and cardiovascular dynamics, cerebrovascular reactivity, and computational approaches to understanding brain function.

I am interested in developing robust, generalizable, and reproducible computational methods that connect artificial intelligence with neuroimaging research.

Research Areas

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, neuroimaging, and physiological modeling.

01 / NEUROIMAGING

Machine Learning for fMRI

Developing computational methods to analyze functional MRI and extract physiologically meaningful information from brain imaging data.

02 / PHYSIOLOGY

Physiological Signal Modeling

Investigating data-driven approaches for estimating respiratory and cardiovascular physiological dynamics from fMRI measurements.

03 / BRAIN HEALTH

AI for Brain Health

Exploring applications of machine learning to brain function, aging, and neurological health through computational neuroimaging.
